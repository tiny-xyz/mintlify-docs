Fees are per-market, taker-only, and computed in integer micro-dollar base
units. The market chooses `fee_rate_bps` at creation; the value is immutable,
must be in `0..=1000`, and appears on every `market_state` card.

<Warning>
Only a zero fee rate produces a zero taker fee on a valid nonempty fill. When a
nonzero raw fee is smaller than the 10-micro-dollar grain, Tiny Exchange
charges one grain. This deliberately differs from the matched venue's
sub-grain-zero behavior.
</Warning>

## Units

| Name | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `t` | Fill price in integer ticks, `1..=999` |
| `qty` | Fill quantity in whole lots |
| `rate_bps` | Market fee rate in basis points, `0..=1000` |
| Base unit | One micro-dollar, `$0.000001` |
| Fee grain | `10` base units, `$0.00001` |
| Winning payout | `1,000,000` base units per lot |

## Taker charge

The unrounded charge is the Polymarket-shaped schedule:

```text
fee = qty × rate × p × (1 - p)
p   = t / 1000
```

In the implementation, the exact intermediate stays in
`10^-4`-micro-dollar units so no division truncates early:

```text
raw_e4 = qty × rate_bps × t × (1000 - t)
```

The charge algorithm is:

```text
if raw_e4 == 0:
    fee_micro = 0
else if raw_e4 < 100_000:
    fee_micro = 10
else:
    fee_micro = floor(raw_e4 / 100_000) × 10
```

The caller applies this function only when the fill's liquidity role is
`taker`. Makers pay `0`, regardless of the market rate.

### Worked charges

| Quantity | Rate | Fill price | Raw fee | Charged fee |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 lots | 400 bps | 600 ticks | 96,000 µUSD | 96,000 µUSD (`$0.096`) |
| 1 lot | 1 bps | 355 ticks | 22.8975 µUSD | 20 µUSD |
| 1 lot | 1 bps | 500 ticks | 25 µUSD | 20 µUSD |
| 1 lot | 1 bps | 999 ticks | 0.0999 µUSD | 10 µUSD minimum |
| 1 lot | 400 bps | 999 ticks | 39.96 µUSD | 30 µUSD |
| any | 0 bps | any valid fill | 0 | 0 |

Fees are symmetric around the midpoint: `t` and `1000 - t` have the same
charge before and after rounding.

## Admission reserve

Every order reserves enough fee collateral for the cash-backed opening lots,
even if the order is expected to make. Fill liquidity is not known until
execution, and an order or later price modification can take.

The worst feasible fill price is the legal price nearest the 500-tick peak of
`t × (1000 - t)`:

```text
buy:  worst_tick = min(limit, 500)
sell: worst_tick = max(limit, 500)
```

For one opening lot, the gateway evaluates `raw_e4` at `worst_tick` and rounds
up to the 10-micro-dollar grain:

```text
reserve_per_lot = ceil(raw_e4 / 100_000) × 10
fee_reserve     = reserve_per_lot × opening_lots
```

The ceiling is load-bearing. It makes the reserve dominate the sum of actual
floored-with-minimum fees across every legal fill slicing.

### Worked reserves

| Order | Rate | Worst tick | Reserve per opening lot |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Buy limit 990 | 400 bps | 500 | 10,000 µUSD |
| Buy limit 300 | 400 bps | 300 | 8,400 µUSD |
| Buy limit 300 | 401 bps | 300 | 8,430 µUSD |
| Sell limit 100 | 400 bps | 500 | 10,000 µUSD |
| Any valid limit | 0 bps | — | 0 |

At 401 bps and 300 ticks, one lot's raw fee is 8,421 µUSD. Admission reserves
8,430 µUSD, while a taker fill at that price charges 8,420 µUSD. The 10 µUSD
difference becomes `fee_refund`.

## Opening and closing lots

Before locking cash, the gateway pledges any unreserved opposite position to
the order as closing lots. The remainder is opening:

```text
opening_lots = order_qty - pledged_closing_lots
required_cash = cost_lock(opening_lots) + fee_reserve(opening_lots)
```

- Opening taker fills fund their fee from the admission reserve. Unused reserve
  becomes `fee_refund`.
- Closing lots reserve neither cost nor fee. Their taker fee is deducted from
  gross exit proceeds.
- A mixed fill consumes closing lots first, then opening lots. Its single taker
  charge uses the opening reserve slice first and takes any remainder from the
  closing proceeds.
- Maker fills charge zero and return the opening fill's entire reserve slice
  through `fee_refund`.

The `1000` bps cap keeps a closing fill's fee within its own proceeds, so a
fully invested account can exit without adding cash. An arithmetic disagreement
is a typed risk fault; the gateway never clamps a fee.

## Client-visible accounting

| Field | When emitted | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `market_state.body.fee_rate_bps` | Snapshot and market updates | Immutable rate used for the market |
| `fill.body.liquidity` | Every fill | Selects maker (`0`) versus taker charge |
| `fill.body.fee` | Every fill | Fee charged now, quoted in micro-dollar base units |
| `fill_settled.body.fee_refund` | Fill finality | Unused opening reserve promoted from `pending_finality` |
| `balance.body.locked` | After money movement | Includes remaining order cost locks and fee reserves |
| `balance.body.pending_finality` | After money movement | Includes fee refunds awaiting finality |

Do not recompute portfolio balances from these components. Apply the newest
absolute balance frame. Recompute fees only for audit and pre-trade estimation.

## Source of truth and test coverage

The arithmetic lives in `protocol/types/src/fees.rs` as `taker_fee` and
`fee_reserve_per_lot`. Gateway property tests prove that reserve slices cover
adversarial fill slicing, that every charge lands on the grain, and that money
conservation includes the fee pot. Parity tests separately pin the deliberate
sub-grain difference from the matched venue.
