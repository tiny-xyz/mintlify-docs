There is no event replay buffer. A reconnect rebuilds an absolute account
baseline, then resumes live delivery.

## Reconnect procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discard connection-local assumptions">
    Treat operator subscriptions and any incomplete snapshot bracket as gone.
    Keep durable client facts such as exchange `order_id` and the original
    bytes of still-retryable orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch or receive a fresh snapshot">
    `GET /v1/orders` returns NDJSON. A WebSocket authentication also stages the
    same baseline. Read from `snapshot_begin` through `snapshot_end` and verify
    the advertised counts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace absolute state">
    Rebuild open orders, positions, market cards, and the three balance
    buckets. Do not merge a partial bracket into the previous baseline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resume live events">
    Apply order events by identity, fills by their global fill identity, and
    balances only when their sequence is newer than the snapshot baseline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Idempotency keys

| State | Application rule |
| --- | --- |
| Fill | Deduplicate by `(market_id, book_seq, fill_index)` |
| Order remaining | Assign the absolute `remaining` value; never subtract `qty` locally |
| Position | Assign absolute `position_yes` and `position_no` from the fill |
| Balance | Highest `balance_seq` wins; sequence numbers are increasing but not dense |
| Market state | Apply the monotone lifecycle `created → open → closed → settled` |
| Fill finality | Join `fill_settled` to the fill by `(market_id, book_seq, fill_index)` |

`ts_ms` is wall-clock observability and never orders events. `fill_settled`
events may arrive out of order across fills and are not watermarks.

## Snapshot contents

An account snapshot contains:

- one `market_state` for each live market in the board baseline;
- one `snapshot_order` per open restable order;
- one `snapshot_position` per market with a nonzero position;
- one `snapshot_balance` with `available`, `locked`, `pending_finality`, and
  its `balance_seq` baseline;
- a final `snapshot_end` whose counts must match the frames received.

The three money buckets are all portfolio value, but only `available` can fund
new orders. `pending_finality` is visible collateral released by fills that is
not spendable until chain finality.

## Terminal order events

An order is terminal when any of these occurs:

- a `fill` sets `remaining` to `"0"`;
- `cancel_done` reports a released remainder;
- `order_expired` reports the market-close sweep;
- `order_rejected` identifies a failed new order or another terminal engine
  refusal.

A rejected modify is not terminal for the underlying order. `modify_done`
changes only price and queue state; the exchange `order_id` and remaining
quantity persist.

## Balance timing around fills

The gateway emits the causing event before its absolute `balance` frame. At a
taker fill, the fee is charged immediately into the venue fee pot. Opening-leg
price improvement and fee over-reserve, plus any net closing proceeds, enter
`pending_finality`. A later `fill_settled` decomposes the promoted amount into
`proceeds`, `price_improvement`, and `fee_refund`.

<Tip>
If a balance frame is missed, do not infer deltas from fills. The next newer
balance frame restates the complete three-bucket truth.
</Tip>
